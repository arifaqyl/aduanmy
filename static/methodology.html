Method & scope

TrafficMY is not an official Rapid KL, Prasarana, or KTMB service. It aggregates recent public rider signals and presents cautious summaries with the evidence attached.

The one rule that matters

A quiet line is not an all-clear. It means no qualifying signal was captured in the status window. Operators do not feed this board. Absence of a report is absence of data — never treat it as confirmation that service is normal.

Not ridership analytics

TrafficMY answers what riders are reporting on the network today — delays, crowding, stuck trains, on the Malaysia calendar day. Passenger-count dashboards answer how many people rode yesterday. Different questions, different products.

How a signal becomes a board entry

  • Public rider posts are collected from the sources below
  • Each is filtered for Malaysian transport relevance and current observable impact
  • Near-duplicates are grouped into a single incident rather than counted repeatedly
  • The incident is matched to a line and summarised — raw wording is not republished
  • Where an official notice covers the same incident, it is marked as corroborated

Reading the evidence meter

████ rider reports matched by an official notice
███░ four or more independent reports
██░░ two or three reports
█░░░ a single unverified report
──── nothing captured today

Sources

  • Threads — primary rider lane when a session is available
  • Reddit, news RSS, and operator notices
  • GTFS for map and station reference only, never as incident truth

Status rules

  • Line status uses the Malaysia calendar day and resets at midnight MYT
  • Longer history is retained for the 14-day baseline, not for live status
  • After the last service of the day a line reads as ended, not as normal
  • Raw posts and usernames are never republished

Known limits

  • Coverage follows where riders post. Quiet lines and off-peak hours are under-observed
  • Report locations are placed by the station mentioned in the post, not by GPS
  • There is no routing engine here — use the official planners for trip planning
