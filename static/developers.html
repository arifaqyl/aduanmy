Live rider intelligence, not ridership charts

TrafficMY answers a different question than an analytics dashboard: what are Malaysian riders reporting on the network right now? Delays, crowding, stuck trains — filtered, summarised, on the Malaysia calendar day.

Not the same as a stats dashboard

Ridership analytics Official DOSM and operator counts. T-1 days. Trends and z-scores. How many people rode yesterday.
TrafficMY signals Live rider posts. Today MYT. Delays and disruptions. What riders say is happening now.

Primary endpoint

GET /api/trafficmy/signals/today?limit=25

Returns the line board plus today's rider signals, each with a glance_line summary in the form time · line · place · issue.

curl -s "/api/trafficmy/signals/today?limit=5"
Press “Try live” to fetch production data.

Where this is useful

  • Property and HR portals — are there delays on the lines near our offices today?
  • Newsrooms and civic tech — rider-ground signal ahead of the operator notice
  • Logistics and field teams — commute risk for Klang Valley dispatch windows
  • Research — a structured, today-only transport corpus with trust metadata attached

Trust and limits

  • A rider signal is not a confirmed disruption. quiet_is_not_all_clear: true always applies.
  • Raw posts and handles are never returned — summaries and source labels only.
  • Write access via POST /api/refresh requires an API key in production.
  • This is a free prototype read tier. There is no SLA and no webhooks yet.
